Three big changes from the original plan

What's new

  • Walk moved to morning (6:30 AM) — steps in before work, sunlight early helps circadian rhythm and vitamin D
  • Training shifted to evening (6:00 PM) — full day of food + recovery before lifting, decompression ritual after work
  • Pre-workout snack at 4:30 PM — fuel for the gym; dinner at 7:30 PM hits the muscle-protein-synthesis window after training

Why this timing works

Supplement chemistry locks the framework. Glutone needs empty stomach — first thing. Zincovit needs food — after breakfast. Shelcal must be separated from zinc — 5+ hours after Zincovit. The 10 AM yogurt gives a calcium top-up far enough from Shelcal that they don't fight each other.

Strength performance peaks 6–8 hours after waking for most people — that puts you right in the sweet spot at 6 PM. Dinner at 7:30 PM clears the 8 PM food cutoff your transformation plan demands.

Non-negotiables

  • 3 meals + 1 snack — no skipping breakfast, no late-night eating
  • 30–40g protein at every meal
  • 3 litres of water (critical with effervescent supplements)
  • Hard 8 PM food cutoff, no exceptions
  • Daily step target hit even on rest days
  • If wheezing appears during walks or training — stop immediately and report to your coach

Locked-in meal

Breakfast & mid-morning

Same breakfast every day. 8 min, one pan. Mid-morning snack hits calcium and protein.

The daily breakfast
⏱ 8 min 🍳 1 pan 💪 ~25g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs (large)
  • 1 slice whole-wheat toast
  • ½ avocado (ripe)
  • 1 tsp butter or olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper, hot sauce optional

Method

  1. Heat butter or olive oil in a small non-stick pan on medium. Don't go hotter.
  2. Crack 2 eggs in. Salt and pepper. Cover with a lid: 2–3 min for sunny-side up with set whites and runny yolk, 4 min for fully cooked.
  3. While eggs cook, toast bread in toaster or dry pan (1 min/side).
  4. Halve avocado, scoop half, mash on toast with a fork. Salt + squeeze of lemon if you have it.
  5. Slide eggs onto avocado toast. Hot sauce optional. Eat immediately.
Why this works: Yolks have vitamin D (rare in food, critical for back pain). Avocado has potassium that balances the sodium from effervescent supplements. Toast = slow-release carbs to push you to 10 AM.

Mid-morning snack · 10 AM

Greek yogurt + fruit
⏱ 2 min🥄 No cook💪 ~18–20g protein

Two ways to do it

  • Plain version: 1 cup Fage 2% Greek yogurt + ½ cup berries + drizzle of honey (optional)
  • Flavored zero-sugar: Single-serve Two Good or Oikos Triple Zero + 1 piece whole fruit (banana, apple, kiwi)

Best fruit pairings

  1. Berries — highest antioxidants, lowest sugar.
  2. Banana — potassium balances sodium from effervescents.
  3. Apple, sliced — fiber, satiety, travels well.
  4. Kiwi or pineapple — high vitamin C; bromelain helps with workout inflammation.
Alternate brands: Plain on training days, flavored zero-sugar on rest days for variety. Avoid grapes and dried fruit daily — concentrated sugar.

Rotate any of these

  • Apple + string cheese — slow-release carb + casein
  • Almonds + small banana — easy desk snack
  • Hard-boiled egg + fruit — best protein-to-cal ratio
  • Single-serve Greek yogurt — if you skipped the 10 AM snack

Lunch · two tacos

Daily Uber Eats with rules. Two tacos with quality protein = ~30–40g protein, decent carbs, enough fat.

Ordering rules

Order these

  • Soft corn or flour tortillas
  • Grilled chicken, steak (carne asada), shrimp, fish
  • Black or pinto beans on the side
  • Guacamole, salsa, pico de gallo
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, cheese
  • Lime wedges

Avoid

  • Hard fried shells (Crunchwraps, taco bowls)
  • Carnitas/al pastor (pork — your restriction)
  • Chips and queso as a side
  • Sour cream loaded up (use guac)
  • Sugary aguas frescas, soda, horchata
  • Margaritas, beer with the meal

7 nights · home-cooked

Dinner recipes

Seven dinners, one per night. All 15–20 min on induction. Hits 35–40g protein target, fits inside 8 PM cutoff, supports recovery from evening training.

Pan-seared chicken + garlic broccoli + riceTraining day
⏱ 20 min💪 ~45g protein🔥 ~520 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast (6–7 oz)
  • 1 cup frozen broccoli florets
  • 1 microwave brown rice pouch
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder
  • Lemon wedge (optional)

Method

  1. Pat the chicken dry with paper towel. Dry surface = better sear. Season both sides with salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder.
  2. Heat olive oil in pan on medium-high (induction level 6–7) for 90 sec until shimmering.
  3. Place chicken in pan. Don't move it. Cook 6–7 min until bottom is golden. Flip once, cook another 5–6 min until 165°F internal or no pink.
  4. While chicken cooks: microwave broccoli in a covered bowl with 2 tbsp water for 3 min. Drain. Toss with garlic, olive oil, salt.
  5. Microwave rice pouch 90 sec.
  6. Rest the chicken on a plate 2 min — locks in juices. Plate everything. Lemon over the chicken.
Post-workout note: Highest-protein dinner of the week. After Workout A (push focus), muscles need amino acids within 2 hours of training. 7:30 PM dinner after 6 PM session = perfect timing.
Pan-seared salmon + quinoa + spinachTraining day
⏱ 18 min💪 ~40g protein🔥 ~580 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 salmon fillet (5–6 oz, skin-on)
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 1 microwave quinoa pouch
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ lemon
  • Salt, pepper, dried dill

Method

  1. Pat salmon dry. Critical for crispy skin. Season both sides with salt + pepper + pinch of dill.
  2. Heat olive oil in pan on medium-high until shimmering. Place salmon skin-side down. Press gently with spatula 15 sec to prevent curling.
  3. Cook 4 min on skin side without moving. Skin releases when ready. Flip, cook 2–3 min for medium.
  4. Transfer salmon to plate. Same pan: toss in garlic 15 sec, add spinach, stir 60–90 sec until wilted. Salt.
  5. Microwave quinoa per packaging.
  6. Plate quinoa, spinach, salmon. Squeeze half lemon over everything.
Recovery bonus: Best dinner of the week for back pain — omega-3s reduce inflammation, vitamin D helps bones, protein supports muscle repair from Workout B.
Ground turkey stir-fry bowlRest day
⏱ 15 min💪 ~38g protein🔥 ~490 cal

Ingredients

  • 6 oz ground turkey (93/7)
  • 1½ cups frozen mixed veggies
  • ½ bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 microwave brown rice pouch
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce or coconut aminos
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Sriracha, green onions

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in pan on medium-high. Add turkey, break it apart, cook 6–8 min until no pink and some bits brown.
  2. Push turkey to one side. Add bell pepper to the empty side, cook 2 min.
  3. Add frozen mixed veggies (no thaw) + garlic. Stir together. Cook 4–5 min.
  4. Pour in soy sauce + sesame oil. Stir to coat, cook 1 more min.
  5. Microwave rice 90 sec.
  6. Plate rice, top with turkey-veggie mix. Sriracha + green onions.
Rest day fit: Lower cal than training-day dinners. Ground turkey is forgiving — overcook and still fine, unlike chicken breast.
Chicken + mushroom sauté + riceTraining day
⏱ 20 min💪 ~42g protein🔥 ~510 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast, sliced thin (½ inch)
  • 1 cup cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • ½ onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 microwave brown rice pouch
  • 1 tbsp olive oil + 1 tsp butter
  • Salt, pepper, dried oregano
  • Optional: 2 tbsp white wine or broth

Method

  1. Slice chicken into thin strips. Thin = fast cook, no dry chicken. Season with salt, pepper, oregano.
  2. Heat olive oil in pan on medium-high. Add chicken in single layer, don't crowd. Cook 2 min/side, remove to plate.
  3. Add butter to same pan. Onions, cook 3 min.
  4. Add mushrooms. Don't stir for 2 min — let them brown. Stir, cook 3 more min until golden.
  5. Garlic 30 sec. Wine or broth (if using) 60 sec to deglaze.
  6. Return chicken, toss, cook 1 more min. Salt to taste.
  7. Microwave rice. Plate rice, top with chicken-mushroom mix.
Vitamin D bonus: Mushrooms are one of few natural food sources of vitamin D — especially UV-treated ones from Whole Foods. Pair with Shelcal D3 for back-pain double dose.
Loaded chicken wrapTraining day · easy
⏱ 12 min💪 ~38g protein🔥 ~520 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast OR leftover Mon chicken
  • 1 large whole-wheat tortilla
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • ¼ cup crumbled feta
  • ¼ avocado, sliced
  • 5–6 cherry tomatoes, halved

One-pan chicken fajita bowlRest day
⏱ 20 min💪 ~44g protein🔥 ~540 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast, sliced thin
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced (mix colors)
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • 1 microwave brown rice pouch
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt (= sour cream)
  • 2 tbsp salsa
  • ¼ avocado, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Fajita mix: 1 tsp cumin + ½ tsp paprika + ½ tsp garlic powder + ½ tsp chili powder + salt

Method

Salmon + steamed vegetables + riceRest day · prep night
⏱ 20 min💪 ~42g protein🔥 ~560 cal

Ingredients

  • 1 salmon fillet (5–6 oz)
  • 1 cup mixed veggies: broccoli, carrots, bell pepper

  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Daily stack

The supplement schedule

Three supplements, three times of day. Glutone needs empty stomach. Zincovit needs food. Shelcal must be separated from zinc to absorb properly.

Glutone 1000 · 6:15 AM
⏱ Empty stomach💊 Effervescent

What's in it

  • Setria L-Glutathione — 600 mg
  • N-Acetylcysteine (NAC) — 400 mg
  • Vitamin C — 40 mg
  • Selenium — 40 mcg

How and why

Drop tablet in 200 ml water, wait until dissolved (~90 sec), drink. Wait 30 min before breakfast. Skin/antioxidant results take 3–4 months — don't expect changes earlier.

Take it before your morning walk: Drink it, walk. By the time you're back, 30-min absorption window is done.
Zincovit · 8:00 AM
⏱ After breakfast💊 Tablet

What's in it

  • Zinc — ~22 mg
  • Vitamin C — 75 mg
  • Selenium — 50 mcg
  • Vitamins A, B-complex, D3, E
  • Magnesium, manganese, copper, iodine

How and why

Swallow whole with full glass of water after breakfast. Never empty stomach — zinc empty causes nausea. This is your safety net multivitamin.

Why we dropped Escor-Z: Zincovit already has vitamin C + zinc + selenium. Stacking Escor-Z pushed zinc to ~32 mg, near the safe ceiling.
Shelcal · 2:30 PM
⏱ After lunch💊 Tablet

What's in it

  • Calcium Carbonate — 1250 mg (= 500 mg elemental Ca)
  • Vitamin D3 — 250 IU

How and why

Swallow whole with water after lunch. 4+ hour gap from Zincovit is critical — calcium and zinc compete for absorption. Too close and neither absorbs well.

For back pain: Calcium alone won't fix it. Need vitamin D blood levels above 30 ng/mL. Shelcal has 250 IU D3 only — consider a vitamin D blood test and possibly a separate D3 2000 IU if deficient.

Side-effect protocol

Kidney & stone protection

Daily effervescents add sodium. Daily vitamin C can metabolize to oxalate. Daily calcium can crystallize in dehydrated urine. All manageable with the right habits.

Four-pillar protocol

1. Hydration

Daily target

  • 3 liters water minimum, spread across day
  • Urine should be pale yellow
  • Extra glass within 30 min of any supplement

Schedule

8 oz on waking · 16 oz before lunch · 16 oz before 5 PM · 16 oz before dinner · 8 oz with dinner · Stop sipping 1 hour before bed.

Carry a 32 oz bottle. Refill twice during the workday = 64 oz; plus meals = 3 L easily.
2. Natural citrate

Sources

  • Lemon water daily (your 6 AM habit)
  • Lime on tacos and dinners
  • Citrus fruit as snack
  • Coconut water 2–3×/week

Why it matters

Citrate is the body's natural stone preventer — binds calcium in urine before it crystallizes. Lemon water habit is doing more work than you realize.

3. Diet rules

Eat liberally

  • Dairy — calcium prevents stones by binding gut oxalate
  • Lean protein, vegetables, whole grains, fruit

Moderate

  • Spinach — high oxalate. 2 cups in a wrap once or twice/week is fine.
  • Coffee — max 2 cups, never within 2 hours of Shelcal.
  • Salt — effervescents add sodium already. Don't pile on.
4. Annual monitoring

Once a year

  • Serum creatinine + eGFR
  • Urine routine
  • Serum calcium + 25-OH-Vitamin D
  • Serum uric acid
  • Ultrasound KUB

See doctor immediately

Sharp one-sided flank/back pain. Blood in urine. Burning urination. Reduced output. Persistent nausea after supplements. Swelling in feet/ankles.

Project Reset bonus: Bundle these tests into the bloodwork visit your coach already wants.

Hard avoids

  • Soda (Coke, Pepsi, Dr. Pepper) — phosphoric acid damages bones, increases stone risk. Zero tolerance.
  • Energy drinks — sodium + caffeine overload on your stack
  • Smoking/vaping — depletes glutathione, makes Glutone supplementation pointless
  • Excessive protein powder — 1 scoop OK, 2–3 daily stresses kidneys
